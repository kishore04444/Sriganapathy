Banana storage and ripening in Coimbatore

Banana storage needs depend on the bunches, their current stage and how they will be used. Sri Ganapathy Banana Commission Mundy offers a place to discuss storage and ripening requirements with the team in Coimbatore.

Explain your timing and intended use

When asking about banana storage, share how long you expect to hold the produce, the stage you need and whether it is for retail or another use. The team can discuss the current options and availability.

Storage is not a one-size-fits-all decision

Different bunches and schedules can call for different handling. Ask about the specific lot and the facility option relevant to it. Availability and suitability should be confirmed directly; no technical performance or shelf-life guarantee is made here.

Talk through ripening stage

If you need bananas at a particular stage, describe the timing and intended use. This helps the team discuss what may be available rather than assuming every stage is always in stock.
